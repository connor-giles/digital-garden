What is a digital garden? How can I use it? What does it look like? 

First and foremost its a digital garden, not a digital library or vault. Ideas should be planted, grown, blossomed, pruned, and when necessary... killed. The garden follows 3 major ideas (tags)

- **Seeds**. Find quality content, seed your digital garden with it to cultivate your curiosity. Do this plant by taking smart personal notes. [[Taking raw notes is useless]]. These seeds, or ideas, do not need to be written in publishable form, they will be cultivated later. *The most important thing is to plant seeds!*
- **Saplings**. Grow upon your existing knowledge by forming new branches, and connecting the dots between these branches (ideas). Write short structured notes, ones that articulate specific ideas, and these are the ones your publish to your digital garden. One note in the garden = one idea. [[Do not keep orphan notes]], [[Threaded thinking instead of linear thinking|thread your thoughts]]
- **Fruits**. The product, the deliverable, that which you are proud of. Essays, videos, complete guides, and more. You might only ever make a couple of these a year or decade. The kind of work you would want others to see from you... [[Long after you are gone|long after you are gone]]

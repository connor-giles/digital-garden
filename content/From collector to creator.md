Many note takers, myself included, hoard content and ideas. It gives them an illusion of knowledge. But what good is knowledge that isn't used. I could not tell you much about the countless notes I've taken at work that I could've sworn I'd use and would be valuable, but since have sat stale and unused in my ever-growing weed garden of information. The best way to truly understand anything is to use the generation effect: [[A gardening guide for your mind|to produce your own content]], in your own words, and to share it with the world to create a feedback loop. Even this note itself was collected from another person's garden, but reinvented in my own words.

- **Collect.** Note-taking, raw-snippets, Kindle highlights + personal commentary.
- **Connect.** Link to previous notes. [[Do not keep orphan notes]]. [[Folders kill creativity|Don't file into folders]]. Every note should have a link
- **Create.** Use the garden, create an original piece of work.

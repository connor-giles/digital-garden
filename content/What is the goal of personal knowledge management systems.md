You hear something new... that stuff is [[data]], your thoughts on that data, thats [[information]]. Knowledge is giving those ideas a way to link to each other, but once those links have been made, that is where the magic happens.

*If [[insight]] is on two ends of the knowledge spectrum, then [[wisdom]] would be the path that links these two idea together.* 

For instance, two things may on the surface have nothing in common, but be present together in the [[knowledge]] spectrum. Being able to connect the two ideas and form that wisdom path opens the door to [[Create original work|creating original work]], as you likely have stumbled across something that others cannot see directly on the surface.

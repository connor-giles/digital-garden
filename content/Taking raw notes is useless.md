Mechanical note-taking, such as listening and passively writing information down is useless for memory, and especially useless for creativity.

Firstly, the [[Generation effect|generation effect]] shows that we remember better when we create our own version of things.

Secondly, creativity requires the ability to connect the dots between seemingly unlinked categories or topics. Raw notes, especially when formatted in[[Folders kill creativity| folder format]], do not allow for discovering surprise connections. 

Therefore it's super important to [[Do not keep orphan notes|not keep orphan notes]]

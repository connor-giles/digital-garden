Due to the old days with filing cabinets, and the existing format of computer operating systems, most humans think and organize in a file or folder set up... *but folders are terrible for creativity*

**What are the issues with folders?**
- Folders assume that an article, or piece of content, fits into a unique, definable category.
- Even worse, you must categorize each piece of content, but what if it kind of fits into two categories? Do you make a copy? Do you change your folders? Theres not an easy solution if you stick with folders.
- Folders at their core imply a rigid hierarchical structure to ideas.
